01. My projects

What I have built

Hi, I'm Varditer. I studied tourism, and these are projects I made on my own: a practice travel project for visitors to Bratislava, and an AI automation that takes care of a weekly routine. Two of them are live, so you can open them. Click a picture to see it bigger.

Practice project · concept and website

KAMAR, a travel service concept for visitors to Bratislava

I made KAMAR to put my tourism studies into practice and to learn how to build a website. The idea: help for Armenian visitors who arrive in Bratislava without the language, such as airport pickup, getting to the hotel and a first walk through the city.

It is a practice project, not a running business. I thought through what such a service could offer, wrote all the website texts, chose the photos and published the site myself, with Claude helping on the code.

Practice project · before the trip

Client form, welcome email and pre-arrival checklist

A good trip starts before the plane lands. For the same project I made a short client form (in Armenian or English) and a welcome email that explains how the service would work.

I also wrote a pre-arrival checklist: hotel and check-in time, how to get out of the airport, local transport, the weather and emergency numbers. Small things, but they are exactly what people worry about on their first day.

AI automation · runs every week

From a manual routine to a weekly report that checks itself

Sorting our family bank statements by hand took time every week. Now Claude reads them from Google Drive, records each payment in the right category and publishes a report. When something looks unusual, such as a transfer between my own accounts or a refund, it writes a note at the top.

I wrote the rules it follows. If there are duplicates, missing entries or totals that don't match, nothing is published, and I get a Slack message explaining what went wrong.
